Roiano, an historic district

The Roiano district (known as Rojàn in Slovenian), located in the city of Trieste, is a valley sloping down through meadows, vineyards, farmland, and small woods toward the built-up area; it is bordered on one side by the Karst escarpment of Opicina and the hills of Gretta (Greta) and Terstenico (Trstenik), the latter more recently known as Monte Radio due to the former presence of EIAR and RAI antennas and facilities, a name still appearing on most maps and on the other by the Scorcola hill facing the city, an area that as evidenced by physical landmarks such as the chapel dedicated to Saint Peter already existed in the year 1000.

The 1804 census recorded only 47 houses and 100 inhabitants, whereas the 1810 census counted more than 100 houses and over 200 inhabitants. The territory of Roiano was divided into various districts: Pischianzi (or Sottomonte), Scala Santa, Case Sparse, Molini, Moreri, Verniellis, Cordaroli, and San Pietro.

The Roiano valley (where the built-up area stands today), known as Val Martinaga, is traversed by four streams: Rio Montorsino, Rio Scalze, Rio dei Molini, and Rio Carbonara, which give their name to the "Piazza tra i Rivi" (a name adopted in 1903); the 19th-century parish church of Saints Hermagoras and Fortunatus is currently located there. The Emo Tarabocchia elementary school was built in 1867; it was one of the first public schools for the general population (as schools had previously been under church control). The school was divided into two sections: one for Slovenian speakers and one for Italian speakers.

Piazza tra i Rivi

Piazza tra i Rivi is a historic square in Roiano, whose name recalls the streams that once flowed through the area. The name “tra i Rivi” means “between the streams” and reflects the presence of watercourses that shaped the neighbourhood before urban development transformed the landscape.

The square is closely connected to the history of Roiano and to nearby Scala Santa, an old route linking Trieste with the Karst plateau. Today, Piazza tra i Rivi remains an important local space and a reminder of Roiano’s past, when streams, rural land and small settlements were a characteristic part of the area.

In piazza tra i Rivi is located the Church of Saints Hermagoras and Fortunatus, which has long been connected to the religious and community life of the neighbourhood. The church reflects the historical importance of Catholic traditions in Trieste. Its presence adds to the character of Roiano and provides another link between the district’s modern urban life and its older history.

Stock S.P.A.

The Stock S.p.A. plant, founded in 1884, was one of Trieste's most important facilities for bottling and producing brandy, grappa, and liqueurs.

The plant was relocated from Via Lionello Stock in Roiano to the city's industrial zone, but in 2012, a decision was made to move operations to the Czech Republic, resulting in the closure of all activities in Trieste and Italy.

The former plant has since been converted into a shopping center that also includes residential units.

Scala Santa

Scala Santa, in the Roiano district of Trieste, is one of the city’s most historic and challenging roads.

For centuries, it served as an important connection between Trieste and the Karst plateau, allowing farmers, traders and travellers to reach the city.

The road climbs for about 2 kilometres, with an average gradient of 16.2%, making it particularly famous for its steepness. Today, Scala Santa is also known for the annual Rampigada Santa, an uphill race held every September. Runners and cyclists compete on the demanding climb, while the event also promotes walking, cycling and sustainable mobility. The race has become a popular local tradition, bringing together sport, community and the history of Trieste.

How to reach Roiano

Roiano is one of Trieste’s most accessible neighbourhoods, thanks to its close connection with the city centre and the main railway station. It can easily be reached by public transport, especially by bus lines 5 and 8. Line 5 connects Roiano with the city centre and continues towards Via Cumano, while line 8 directly links Roiano with Piazza della Libertà and Trieste Centrale railway station, before continuing towards Valmaura.

The neighbourhood is only a short distance from Trieste Centrale station, making it convenient for visitors arriving by train. Roiano has also recently benefited from improvements to its parking facilities. In November 2024, a new 99-space car park opened on the former Polstrada site, with entrances from Via dei Moreri and Via Montorsino. The parking area is part of a larger urban redevelopment project that also created green spaces, a playground and other community facilities.

These transport links and new facilities make Roiano easy to reach by bus, train or car, while also connecting the neighbourhood more closely to the rest of Trieste.
