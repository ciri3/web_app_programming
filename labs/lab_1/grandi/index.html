Roiano: The Heart of Italian Kitchen

Explore Roiano, a cozy culinary haven where heritage, creativity, and community come together on every plate.

Nestled between the hills and the sea in Trieste, the historic district of Roiano boasts a rich and fascinating gastronomic culture. The food scene here mirrors the diverse historical influences of the region, blending traditional Austrian-Hungarian heritage, Venetian maritime flavors, and classic Italian culinary artistry. Roiano's local cuisine is centered around warmth, community, and the sharing of hearty, comforting meals. Historic neighborhood trattorias, family-run osterias dating back decades, and local gathering spots reflect a deep-rooted passion for fresh seafood, aromatic herbs, rich cheeses, and comforting homemade pasta dishes. Whether it is a casual weeknight dinner or a festive weekend celebration, the traditional and creative dishes found in Roiano tell a story of a tight-knit neighborhood that deeply values authentic flavors and genuine hospitality.

Sandro Castelli's Pizza

Description

The Pizza of Sandro Castelli is a standout culinary creation deeply appreciated within the vibrant social fabric of Roiano. Crafted with meticulous attention to detail, this pizza represents the pinnacle of modern artisanal pizza-making combined with local neighborhood warmth. It is celebrated for its perfect balance between a crisp yet tender texture and an explosion of rich, high-quality flavors. Each bite offers a delightful contrast between the airy, golden crust and the melting toppings, making it a beloved local favorite that captures the true essence of Italian comfort food perfected through personal dedication.

Preparation

The preparation begins with the dough, which undergoes a long, slow fermentation process to ensure it is exceptionally light, airy, and easily digestible. Once rested, the dough is hand-stretched with care to form a distinct, puffy, and golden cornicione. It is then dressed with a vibrant base of San Marzano tomato sauce, bursting with natural sweetness and bright acidity, and layered with creamy, high-quality buffalo mozzarella sourced fresh from artisanal dairies. Baked piping hot in a traditional wood-fired oven until the crust blisters and the cheese bubbles, the pizza is finished right out of the oven with fresh basil leaves, a drizzle of robust extra virgin olive oil, and delicate shavings of aged Parmigiano Reggiano.

The Secret 48-Hour Hydration

While traditional Neapolitan pizza relies on a standard fermentation, Sandro Castelli’s recipe uses an ultra-high hydration dough (around 75-80%) that ferments in cold storage for a full 48 hours. This prolonged, cold fermentation breaks down complex starches completely, making the pizza remarkably light and airy—so much so that locals famously joke you can eat a whole one by yourself and still feel as light as a feather afterwards!

Andrea De Lorenzo's Pasta al Salmone

Description

The Andrea De Lorenzo's Pasta al Salmone is an exquisite and sophisticated dish that bridges the gap between coastal freshness and rich, velvety culinary indulgence. It highlights the delicate yet distinct flavor of fresh seafood combined with a luxurious, comforting sauce that coats every strand of pasta. This dish offers a magnificent symphony of rich, savory, and aromatic flavors, leaving a lasting impression of elegance and culinary refinement that appeals to both seafood lovers and connoisseurs of comforting home cooking.

Preparation

The dish starts with top-grade artisanal egg tagliatelle or delicate gourmet pasta cooked al dente. In a large skillet, finely minced shallots are gently sweated in high-quality extra virgin olive oil until translucent, then deglazed with a splash of crisp white wine to lift the savory notes. Premium-grade fresh salmon fillets are gently pan-seared and flaked into the pan to integrate with the aromatics. A generous splash of fresh heavy cream (or a touch of rich mascarpone) is then incorporated, melding seamlessly with the natural juices of the salmon to create a luxuriously smooth and coating sauce. Finally, the dish is tossed together with the pasta and finished with a handful of freshly chopped flat-leaf parsley, cracked black pepper, and a hint of fresh lemon zest to brighten the palate.

The Coastal-Alpine Fusion

Although fresh salmon is typically associated with Nordic or North American cuisine, Andrea De Lorenzo’s recipe cleverly bridges Trieste’s maritime heritage with the rich, dairy-heavy culinary traditions of the nearby Alpine foothills. By pairing delicate river or sea salmon with a luxurious cream and white wine reduction, the dish reflects Trieste’s historical identity as a cultural crossroads where Mediterranean seafood meets Central European comfort food.

Elisabetta Grandi's Gnocchi di zucca con gorgonzola e pancetta

Description

Elisabetta Grandi's Gnocchi di zucca con Gorgonzola e Pancetta is a masterclass in autumnal comfort food, skillfully combining earthy sweetness with bold, savory richness. The foundation of the dish lies in its pillowy, handcrafted dumplings that practically melt on the tongue, offering a delicate sweetness that beautifully balances the heavier, punchier elements of the recipe. It is a warm, deeply satisfying culinary embrace that showcases the absolute best of regional home-style cooking with a creative and decadent twist.

Preparation

The process begins by roasting sweet orange pumpkin pulp until tender, which is then mashed and folded into soft flour and fresh potatoes to form a smooth dough. The dough is rolled and cut into delicate gnocchi dumplings, which are boiled until they float to the surface. Meanwhile, the sauce is prepared by rendering crisp, golden cubes of smoked pancetta in a skillet to achieve an irresistible crunch and a deep, savory aroma. In a separate pan, a luxurious cream of aged Gorgonzola cheese is melted gently to create a sharp, creamy base. The cooked gnocchi are tossed directly into the Gorgonzola sauce, topped with the crispy pancetta, and finished with a sprinkling of toasted crushed walnuts for texture and a dash of freshly grated nutmeg.

The Balance of Contrast

In traditional Italian gastronomy, combining sweet pumpkin with a pungent, blue-veined cheese like Gorgonzola was historically considered a daring culinary risk. Elisabetta Grandi’s recipe masterfully turns this contrast into its greatest strength: the natural, earthy sweetness of the roasted pumpkin acts as a natural buffer, taming the sharp bite of the Gorgonzola while the smoked pancetta adds a smoky depth that unites the entire flavor profile.
